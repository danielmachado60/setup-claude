# Locale: fr (France)

Market knowledge for projects with `Language: fr` on line 4 of ABOUT.md. Read this
before any client-facing work — copy, UI, forms, checkout, SEO. Anchored on France;
Belgium/Switzerland/Francophone Africa share the language but differ on payment rails
and legal specifics — confirm the target country before assuming either.

## Review mining (customer-voice research)

- Primary sources: Google Maps reviews (dominant for local businesses), TripAdvisor for
  hospitality/restaurants, **Avis Vérifiés** (AFNOR-certified reviews — a recognized
  French trust badge, common on e-commerce sites and worth displaying near conversion
  points if the project has it).

## Legal requirements (stricter than most markets)

- GDPR ("RGPD") applies — CNIL (the French authority) is known for strict enforcement
  and has fined sites over dark-pattern cookie banners. The accept/reject choice must be
  genuinely equal (same size/prominence for both buttons), never a disguised "reject".
- **Mentions légales** ("legal notice") is a distinct, legally required page in France —
  separate from the privacy policy — identifying the site publisher, host and contact
  details. Plan this page into every French site; its absence is a real compliance gap,
  not a nice-to-have.

## Payment rails

- Cards (Carte Bancaire/CB) are the dominant default — no PIX/Bizum-equivalent is widely
  adopted. PayPal is broadly trusted. Buy-now-pay-later via Alma or Klarna is growing for
  higher-ticket purchases — worth offering if relevant to the project's price point.

## WhatsApp and contact channels

- WhatsApp is far less dominant as a primary CTA in France than in Brazil or Latin
  America — French consumers still default to contact forms, phone or email. Don't
  assume a floating WhatsApp button reads as normal; a clear contact form usually
  converts better unless the client's audience skews younger/digital-native.

## Formats

- Dates: DD/MM/YYYY. Currency: 1 234,56 € (space as thousands separator, comma decimal,
  symbol AFTER the number — do not default to the US $1,234.56 pattern). Phone: +33 X XX
  XX XX XX (written 0X XX XX XX XX domestically).

## Tone notes for fr copy

- **"Vous" is the default, safe professional register** — unlike Spanish/Portuguese,
  formal address is expected by default in French business copy. Using "tu" is a
  deliberate, risky brand choice (youth-oriented or intentionally casual brands only) —
  confirm with the client before switching registers; do not assume casual tone is safe.
- Avoid corporate jargon translated literally from English ("solutions", "synergie") —
  mined customer vocabulary (real reviews, real complaints) always beats institutional
  clichés, same principle as every other market.

## Local SEO

- Google is dominant (90%+ share). French consumers research extensively before buying —
  a complete, transparent "Mentions légales" + clear return/refund policy pages
  measurably help conversion, not just compliance.
