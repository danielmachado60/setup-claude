# Locale: es (Spanish — Spain-anchored, Latin America notes)

Market knowledge for projects with `Language: es` on line 4 of ABOUT.md. Read this
before any client-facing work — copy, UI, forms, checkout, SEO.

**Scope note:** "Spanish" spans very different legal/payment systems across countries.
This file anchors on Spain (one coherent EU market) and flags where Latin America
diverges — confirm the specific country with the client before assuming a payment rail,
legal requirement or slang; do not default to Mexico or Argentina without asking.

## Review mining (customer-voice research)

- Spain: Google Maps reviews dominant; TripAdvisor for hospitality; general e-commerce
  trust badges less standardized than in France/Germany.
- Latin America: Google Maps + Facebook reviews both carry weight; in some markets
  (Mexico, Argentina, Colombia) Mercado Libre seller reviews matter for e-commerce.

## Data protection

- Spain/EU: GDPR ("RGPD") applies — AEPD (the Spanish authority) enforces cookie consent
  strictly; require a genuine accept/reject choice, no pre-checked boxes, and a linked
  privacy policy ("Política de Privacidad").
- Latin America: each country has its own law (Mexico's LFPDPPP, Argentina's Ley de
  Protección de Datos Personales, Colombia's Habeas Data...) — do not assume GDPR-level
  requirements apply; confirm which country's law governs the project.

## Payment rails

- Spain: cards dominant; **Bizum** (instant P2P bank transfer) is a strong secondary
  option, especially for smaller purchases — worth listing if the payment provider
  supports it.
- Latin America: fragmented by country — Mexico (OXXO cash vouchers, SPEI transfers),
  Argentina (Mercado Pago is close to ubiquitous; high inflation makes "cuotas sin
  interés"/interest-free installments a major conversion lever), Colombia (PSE bank
  transfers), Chile (WebPay). Never assume one rail — ask which country/gateway.

## WhatsApp and contact channels

- Across most of Latin America (especially Mexico), WhatsApp Business is a primary,
  expected CTA channel — a floating WhatsApp button reads as normal, not tacky.
- In Spain, WhatsApp is common but less dominant than in LatAm — forms and phone still
  convert well, especially for B2B/institutional audiences.

## Formats

- Spain: dates DD/MM/YYYY; currency 1.234,56 € (dot thousands, comma decimal, symbol
  after); phone +34 XXX XX XX XX.
- Latin America: date/currency/phone formats vary by country — confirm before hardcoding
  a format in forms or invoices.

## Tone notes

- Spain: "tú" is common for consumer/younger brands, "usted" for institutional/B2B —
  pick one deliberately and stay consistent.
- Argentina: **voseo** ("vos" instead of "tú") is the norm in everyday and marketing
  copy — using "tú" reads as foreign/stiff to an Argentine audience. Confirm the target
  country before writing copy with "tú" vs "vos".
- Avoid literal translation of idioms between Spain and Latin America — mined customer
  vocabulary (real reviews, real complaints) beats a translated version of English/PT copy.

## Local SEO

- Google is dominant across the entire Spanish-speaking world (95%+ share).
- Google Business Profile matters heavily for local intent in both Spain and Latin
  America — keep NAP (name, address, phone) consistent between the site and the profile.
