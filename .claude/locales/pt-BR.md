# Locale: pt-BR (Brazil)

Market knowledge for projects with `Language: pt-BR` on line 4 of ABOUT.md. Read this
before any client-facing work — copy, UI, forms, checkout, SEO.

## Review mining (customer-voice research)

- Primary sources: Google Maps reviews (dominant for local businesses), Reclame Aqui
  (complaints platform with huge cultural weight — mine it for objections, failure modes
  and the exact words angry customers use), Trustpilot BR (growing in e-commerce).
- A good Reclame Aqui score ("Ótimo"/RA1000) is a trust asset — surface it near conversion
  points. A bad score: never link to it, and address the top complaints in the copy.

## LGPD (Brazilian data-protection law) for sites and forms

- Every form collecting personal data needs a stated purpose and a link to a privacy
  policy page ("Política de Privacidade") — plan that page into every site.
- Marketing consent must be explicit: unchecked opt-in checkbox, never pre-checked.
- Cookie banner is required when analytics/ads trackers run; prefer a discreet banner
  with an accept action and a policy link — blocking walls hurt conversion.

## Payment rails

- PIX is ubiquitous: instant bank transfer, free for the buyer, expected at every
  checkout — usually with a discount ("5% de desconto no PIX"). List it first.
- Boleto bancário still matters for unbanked/older audiences (cash payment, 1–3 business
  days to clear — plan order status around that delay).
- Parcelamento (credit-card installments, "12x sem juros") is a cultural expectation for
  anything above ~R$100 — always show the installment price next to the full price.

## WhatsApp as the dominant CTA channel

- For most local-business sites the primary CTA is a WhatsApp link, not a form:
  `https://wa.me/55DDNUMBER?text=...` with a pre-filled message giving context
  ("Olá! Vi o site e quero um orçamento").
- A floating WhatsApp button is expected, not tacky, on Brazilian local-business sites.
- Forms still convert for B2B/institutional audiences — offer both when in doubt.

## Formats

- Dates: DD/MM/YYYY (15/09/2026). Currency: R$ 1.234,56 (dot for thousands, comma for
  decimals). Phones: +55 (11) 91234-5678 — mobile numbers have 9 digits starting with 9.
- Addresses revolve around the CEP (postal code) — use ViaCEP autofill in checkout and
  registration forms.

## Tone notes for pt-BR copy

- Use "você" (never "tu" for a national audience); imperative CTAs ("Peça seu orçamento").
- Avoid corporate jargon — "soluções", "sinergia", "há mais de X anos no mercado" — mined
  customer vocabulary always beats institutional clichés.
- Urgency claims must be real: Brazilian consumers are highly promotion-literate
  (Black Friday scars — "metade do dobro" is a running national joke about fake discounts).

## Local SEO

- Google is effectively the only search engine that matters in Brazil (95%+ share).
- Google Business Profile often outranks the site itself for local intent — keep NAP
  (name, address, phone) consistent between site and profile.
- Reclame Aqui pages rank for "<brand> reclamação" queries and directly affect
  conversion — reputation there is part of the SEO/conversion picture.
